# CFO — Unit Economics Pack

## Measurement bridge
Gross order value is GST-inclusive. Net sales remove the illustrative 5% GST component and refunds. Contribution then deducts estimated COGS, shipping subsidy and a ₹65 variable fulfilment/handling assumption per delivered order. Marketing is deducted below contribution to create a contribution-after-marketing view.

## Core controls
- Exclude RTO orders from delivered sales.
- Track refunds separately and avoid subtracting them twice.
- Compare contribution per delivered order by discount band, payment method and channel.
- Review festive months for volume growth versus margin dilution.

## Scenario model
The Excel workbook allows COD share reduction and RTO-rate changes. It estimates avoided RTOs and incremental contribution using transparent assumptions. It is a sensitivity model, not a forecast.

## Caveats
GST rate, fulfilment cost and COGS are illustrative. Actual accounting treatment requires company-specific finance and tax validation.
