# Portfolio Presentation — 12 Slides

1. **Title:** End-to-End E-commerce Analytics for an Indian D2C Brand — decision-led analytics from click to contribution.
2. **Business context:** Vastraa, channels, stakeholders and profitable growth objective.
3. **Data architecture:** synthetic sources → raw → staging → mart → Python/Power BI.
4. **Data quality:** bots, duplicate events, missing UTMs, late arrivals, outliers and controls.
5. **Executive scorecard:** orders, delivered sales, contribution, margin, AOV and RTO.
6. **Product funnel:** mobile vs desktop, payment-step friction and journey recommendations.
7. **Customer value:** cohorts, retention, RFM segments and repeat-purchase behaviour.
8. **Marketing effectiveness:** CAC/ROAS, channel quality and four attribution views.
9. **Operations & finance:** COD/RTO by tier, contribution bridge and festive margin dip.
10. **Experimentation:** pre-registration, SRM, confidence intervals and ship/iterate/kill decisions.
11. **Predictive analytics:** repeat-purchase propensity and seasonal demand baseline; limitations.
12. **Executive action plan:** mobile checkout, COD controls, contribution-led channel allocation and discount governance.
