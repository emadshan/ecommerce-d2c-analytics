# Mock Stakeholder Q&A

**CEO: Why not optimize GMV?**  
Because GMV includes undelivered and returned orders and ignores COGS, fulfilment and acquisition costs. Delivered net sales and contribution are closer to profitable growth.

**CMO: Should we cut branded search?**  
No. Protect coverage for high-intent demand, but do not assume last-click ROAS is incremental. Use holdouts and new-customer contribution to set marginal budgets.

**Product: Is mobile the only issue?**  
The synthetic data plants a payment handoff friction point. Validate with real device telemetry, payment failure codes and usability research before prioritizing implementation.

**CFO: Can we use the Excel output as a forecast?**  
No. It is a scenario calculator with explicit assumptions. Replace assumptions with actual variable costs, GST treatment, refunds and payment fees before planning.

**Operations: Should we disable COD in tier-3?**  
The synthetic baseline is 32.1% for tier-3 COD versus 4.0% across tier-3 prepaid methods. Pilot risk-based COD confirmation, prepaid incentives and selective limits; measure incremental delivered contribution and customer impact.

**CEO: Are the experiment results real?**  
No. They are synthetic randomized outcomes designed to demonstrate a complete experiment workflow. Real decisions require a properly powered test and production guardrail monitoring.
