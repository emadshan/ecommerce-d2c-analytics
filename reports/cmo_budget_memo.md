# CMO — Channel Budget Reallocation

## Recommendation
Do not scale branded search solely because its CAC and ROAS look attractive. It captures existing intent and can receive last-click credit for demand created elsewhere. Paid social can appear expensive at first order but may produce higher repeat contribution.

## Observed channel cohort comparison
- Paid social CAC: ₹4,895; repeat-customer share: 75.0%.
- Branded search CAC: ₹2,140; repeat-customer share: 44.7%.
- These are synthetic cohort patterns; branded search's apparent efficiency should not be mistaken for incremental acquisition.

## Proposed allocation process
- Protect branded search coverage but cap spend at marginal efficiency.
- Evaluate paid social on 90/180-day contribution LTV:CAC, not first-order ROAS.
- Keep non-brand search as a controlled acquisition test.
- Require holdouts or geo experiments before declaring incremental channel value.
- Reconcile marketplace sales and paid marketplace spend to avoid double counting.

## Measurement
Compare first-touch, last-touch, linear and position-based attribution. Use new-customer CAC and repeat purchase by acquisition channel. Treat the models as alternative credit rules, not causal estimates.
