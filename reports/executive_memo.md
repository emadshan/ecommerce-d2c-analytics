# Vastraa — Executive Decision Memo

**To:** CEO and Leadership Team  
**From:** Analytics  
**Subject:** Profitable growth priorities  
**Period:** 18-month synthetic case study

## Decisions requested
1. Manage delivered net sales and contribution, not GMV alone.
2. Rebalance acquisition measurement toward new-customer and repeat contribution.
3. Reduce tier-3 COD RTO through risk-based payment nudges and confirmation.
4. Prioritize the mobile payment handoff in the checkout roadmap.
5. Govern festive discounting against contribution floors.

## Why now
The analysis framework connects customer acquisition, product conversion, fulfilment outcomes and financial contribution in one reproducible model. It exposes how apparently efficient channels can capture existing demand and how volume spikes can hide margin dilution.

## 30/60/90-day plan
- **30 days:** instrument checkout errors, establish channel/customer cohort reporting, publish RTO baseline.
- **60 days:** run mobile checkout and tier-3 COD interventions with randomized holdouts.
- **90 days:** move channel budget reviews to contribution LTV:CAC and launch discount guardrails.

## Risks and caveats
All data is synthetic; modelled effects are planted by design. The project demonstrates analytical method and stakeholder communication, not evidence about a real brand.
