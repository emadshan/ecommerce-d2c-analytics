# CEO — State of the Business

## Executive view
Vastraa's synthetic demand profile shows strong seasonal peaks, a meaningful mobile traffic mix and material operational leakage from COD/RTO. Headline GMV is not an adequate growth scorecard: decision-making should prioritize delivered net sales and contribution after fulfilment and acquisition costs.

## Measured synthetic baseline
- 60,000 orders; 53,438 delivered orders; 10.9% RTO rate.
- ₹11.98 crore net sales and ₹5.97 crore contribution.
- Overall contribution margin: 49.9%; AOV: ₹2,449.
- Festive-month contribution margin averages 43.3%, versus 52.1% in other months.

## What matters
1. Separate delivered orders from placed orders; RTO is not revenue.
2. Evaluate channels on new-customer contribution and repeat behaviour, not platform ROAS alone.
3. Treat festive sales as a margin management problem: promotions lift volume but can dilute contribution.
4. Prioritize mobile checkout improvements and COD risk controls as cross-functional growth levers.

## CEO actions
- Make contribution margin and delivered-customer growth the top-line scorecard.
- Require channel budget proposals to include new-customer share and repeat contribution.
- Set a tier-3 COD experiment with prepaid incentives and confirmation workflows.
- Review discount depth against incremental contribution during festive campaigns.
