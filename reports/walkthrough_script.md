# Five-Minute Portfolio Walkthrough

**0:00–0:40 — Problem.** “I built this case study around a D2C fashion brand that needs profitable growth. The same growth decision affects marketing, product, finance and operations.”

**0:40–1:20 — Data.** “The Python generator creates 18 months of reproducible Indian commerce data. I deliberately included bot traffic, duplicates, missing campaign tags, late events and inconsistent city values so the pipeline demonstrates quality controls.”

**1:20–2:00 — Engineering.** “Data moves through raw, staging and mart layers. PostgreSQL provides the analytical model; BigQuery SQL demonstrates partitioning and clustering for event-scale workloads.”

**2:00–2:50 — Product and marketing.** “I compare mobile and desktop funnel steps, cohort retention and RFM groups. On marketing, I compare CAC and ROAS with four attribution models, while explaining why branded search may capture existing intent.”

**2:50–3:40 — Finance and operations.** “I bridge GST-inclusive order value to net sales and contribution. Then I isolate RTO risk by city tier and payment method, and use Excel to model a COD-share reduction.”

**3:40–4:30 — Experiments and prediction.** “I pre-register primary and guardrail metrics, check assignment balance, estimate confidence intervals and make ship/iterate/kill calls. I also build a lean repeat-purchase model and seasonal demand baseline.”

**4:30–5:00 — Decisions.** “The recommendations are mobile checkout fixes, tier-3 COD interventions, contribution-led channel budgets and festive discount guardrails. The key point is that I connect analysis to a decision and its financial consequence.”
