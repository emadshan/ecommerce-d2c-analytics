# KPI Dictionary

| KPI | Formula | Grain | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orders | Count distinct order_id | Order/day | CEO |
| Delivered rate | Delivered orders / all orders | Order/month | Ops |
| RTO rate | RTO orders / all orders | Order × city tier × payment | Ops |
| AOV | Gross order value / orders | Order/month | CEO |
| Net sales | Delivered gross / (1+GST) less refunds net of GST | Order | CFO |
| Contribution | Net sales − COGS − shipping − variable fulfilment cost | Order | CFO |
| Contribution margin | Contribution / net sales | Month | CFO |
| CAC | Channel spend / new customers attributed | Channel/month | CMO |
| ROAS | Attributed net sales / channel spend | Channel/month | CMO |
| Funnel conversion | Unique sessions at step / unique sessions at entry | Session/device | Product |
| Repeat rate | Customers with 2+ delivered orders / delivered customers | Customer/cohort | CRM |
| Retention | Cohort customers active in month N / cohort size | Cohort/month offset | Product |
| LTV:CAC | Expected contribution LTV / CAC | Customer/channel | CFO |
| Payback | CAC / monthly contribution per acquired customer | Channel/cohort | CFO |
| Return rate | Returned delivered orders / delivered orders | Order/month | Ops |
