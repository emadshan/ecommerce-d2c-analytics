# Project Charter

## Objective
Find the drivers of profitable growth for Vastraa, a fictional Indian D2C apparel brand operating a website and marketplaces.

## Scope
18 months of synthetic order, customer, product, return, event, campaign, spend and experiment data. Work spans raw ingestion, staging, marts, quality, commercial analytics, experimentation, modelling and BI.

## Success criteria
1. Reproducible generator with fixed seed.
2. Traceable raw → staging → mart transformations.
3. Business KPIs reconcile to documented definitions.
4. Planted business effects are detected and explained with caveats.
5. Outputs are decision-oriented and reproducible on a laptop.

## Out of scope
Real payment gateway integration, real customer data, tax/legal advice, production-grade identity resolution and causal claims from observational attribution.

## Delivery principles
Use INR, document grain and assumptions, retain raw source data, profile before cleaning, and distinguish correlation from causation.
