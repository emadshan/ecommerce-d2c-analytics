# Stakeholder Personas

| Stakeholder | Decision | Needs | Primary deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| CEO | Where to invest for profitable growth? | Revenue, contribution, repeat rate, risk | State-of-business brief |
| CMO | Which channels deserve incremental budget? | CAC, ROAS, incrementality caveats, cohort quality | Budget reallocation memo |
| Head of Product | Which funnel step blocks conversion? | Device funnel, journeys, cohorts, experiments | Product insights memo |
| CFO | Is growth creating cash and contribution? | Net sales, COGS, refunds, logistics, CAC, payback | Financial pack + scenario model |
| Operations Head | How to reduce delivery leakage? | COD, RTO, city tier, payment mix | RTO analysis and recommendations |
